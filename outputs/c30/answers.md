## What is the exit load of HSBC Value Fund?

**Answer:** The exit load for the HSBC Value Fund is as follows:

*   If units redeemed or switched out are up to 10% of the units purchased or switched in within 1 year from the date of allotment, the exit load is Nil [1, 2].
*   If units redeemed or switched out are over and above the 10% limit within 1 year from the date of allotment, the exit load is 1% [2].
*   If units are redeemed or switched out on or after 1 year from the date of allotment, the exit load is Nil [2].

Additionally, no exit load is chargeable for switches between different options of the scheme, for units allotted on account of IDCW reinvestments, or for segregated portfolios [2].
