# Advisor FAQ

> Simulated internal document written for the VerifAI demo. The firm and its rules are fictional.

## Which documents count as approved?

Only documents in the VerifAI knowledge base are approved for client communication. Compliance adds a document after review and records who approved it and when. Material from news sites, social media or chat groups is not approved.

## How often are fund factsheets updated?

Fund houses publish factsheets every month. Compliance uploads the new factsheet within 3 working days of publication and marks the previous month's version as superseded. Always quote figures from the latest factsheet.

## Can I share a factsheet directly with a client?

Yes. You may share the full, unedited PDF of an approved factsheet. Do not share screenshots of single pages, because they remove the mandatory disclaimers.

## What do I do if VerifAI cannot answer a question?

Do not guess. Tell the client you will confirm the information, then raise a request with the compliance desk at compliance-desk@verifai-demo.example. The desk replies within 1 working day.

## Can I tell a client which fund to buy?

No. Advisors at this firm share factual information from approved documents. Personalised recommendations require a separate suitability assessment, which only certified investment advisers may carry out.
