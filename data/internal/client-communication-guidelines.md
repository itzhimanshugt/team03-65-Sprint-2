# Client Communication Guidelines

> Simulated internal document written for the VerifAI demo. The firm and its rules are fictional.

## 1. Quoting numbers

Quote expense ratios, exit loads, NAVs and returns exactly as written in the latest approved document. Always state the date of the document, for example: "As per the factsheet dated 31 August 2026".

## 2. Past performance

When you mention past returns, add this sentence: "Past performance may or may not be sustained in future and is not a guarantee of future returns."

## 3. Risk level

When you discuss a scheme, state its riskometer level (Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High or Very High) from the latest factsheet or KIM.

## 4. Forbidden phrases

Never use the phrases "guaranteed returns", "risk-free", "sure-shot" or "can't lose" for any mutual fund scheme.

## 5. Written records

Send every factual answer about a scheme to the client in writing (email) within 24 hours of a phone call, with the source document named.

## 6. Escalation

Escalate any complaint about mis-selling to the compliance desk on the same working day.
